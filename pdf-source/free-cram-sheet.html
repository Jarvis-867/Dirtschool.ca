IUOE APTITUDE TEST - 10 MINUTE CRAM SHEET

All formulas, conversions, and exam traps on one page

MATH FORMULAS

  • Percent Grade = (Rise / Run) x 100
  • Bank Cubic Yards (BCY) = Loose Cubic Yards (LCY) ÷ (1 + swell %)
  • Example: 125 LCY at 25% swell = 125 ÷ 1.25 = 100 BCY
  • Cut/Fill Volume = Length x Width x (Cut or Fill Depth)
  • Measured in feet? Divide by 27 for cubic yards
  • Equipment Production = Volume / Time
  • Fuel Consumption (L/hr) = Fuel Used / Hours Operated

HYDRAULIC PRESSURE

  • 1 PSI = 6.89 kPa
  • 1 Bar = 14.5 PSI

HYDRAULIC FLOW

  • Flow Rate = Pump Displacement x RPM
  • GPM = Displacement (cu in/rev) x RPM ÷ 231
  • L/min = Displacement (cc/rev) x RPM ÷ 1,000

COMMON UNIT CONVERSIONS

  • 1 yard = 3 feet
  • 1 cubic yard = 27 cubic feet
  • 1 mile = 5,280 feet
  • 1 US gallon = 3.785 liters  |  1 imperial gallon = 4.546 liters
  • 1 short ton (US) = 2,000 pounds  |  1 metric tonne = 1,000 kg ≈ 2,205 pounds

EXAM TRAPS TO AVOID

  • - Watch unit conversions (cubic yards vs feet)
  • - Percent grade is RISE/RUN, not vice versa
  • - Mechanical questions test your reasoning, not memory
  • - Time management: move on if stuck for 2+ minutes
  • - Read questions twice - look for "NOT" or "EXCEPT"

EQUIPMENT SPECS TO KNOW

  • - Excavator bucket sizes: 0.5 to 3+ cubic yards
  • - Dozer blade capacities: about 2 to 45 cubic yards (5 to 15 typical for mid-size, D6-D8)
  • - Loader bucket: 2 to 5 cubic yards
  • - Grader: 12 to 16 foot blade on most road graders; up to 24 ft on mining graders
