Cut & Fill Volume Calculator

The fastest way to calculate earthwork volume:

Formula: Width (ft) × Length (ft) × Depth (ft) ÷ 27 = Cubic Yards

Example: A cut that's 100 ft wide, 200 ft long, and 5 ft deep:

  • 100 × 200 × 5 = 100,000 cubic feet
  • 100,000 ÷ 27 = 3,703 cubic yards

No pencil. No notepad. No guessing.

Trench Volume Calculator

Same principle, different scenario. Whether you're digging trenches for utilities or drainage:

Formula: Width × Depth × Length ÷ 27 = Cubic Yards

Example: A 3-ft-wide, 6-ft-deep trench running 500 feet:

  • 3 × 6 × 500 = 9,000 cubic feet
  • 9,000 ÷ 27 = 333 cubic yards

Pre-Trip Checklist

Ten essential checks every morning, before you fire up the engine:

  1. Engine start & idle
  2. Hydraulic fluid level
  3. Bucket/attachment function
  4. Blade/bucket condition
  5. Tire/track condition
  6. Lights working
  7. Mirrors clear
  8. Seat belt functional
  9. Emergency stop working
  10. Fuel level

Tap boxes as you check them. Timestamps everything. No paper required.
